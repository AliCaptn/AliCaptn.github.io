Why businesses choose AR BI Consulting

Reliable numbers, plain language and solutions that fit how you work. Here is what you can expect from us.

4.7+ years of experience

Hands-on Business Intelligence delivery, not theory. Over 4.7 years we have worked across dashboards, data models, SQL, DAX and reporting automation, so we know where projects usually go wrong and how to avoid it.

Customized solutions

Nothing is forced into a template. We start from your process, your data and your people, then design the dashboard or model to fit.

Business-focused approach

We start with the decision you need to make, then work back to the data. That keeps every chart and measure tied to a reason.

Reliable communication

You get clear updates and straight answers throughout. Review points at the end of each stage keep the project on track and free of surprises.

Data accuracy

Figures are reconciled to the source before you see them. When two numbers disagree, we find out why and document the answer.

Professional reporting

Clean, consistent layouts that leadership can read at a glance, with documentation written for the people who use the reports.

Scalable solutions

Models and reports are built so they grow with your questions, without needing a rebuild every time something changes.

What you can expect

  • A short discovery conversation about goals, decisions and data
  • Layout and logic approved by you before the build
  • Figures checked against your source data
  • A walkthrough and written documentation at handover
  • Straight answers when something is unclear or out of scope

What we will not do

  • Promise results we cannot verify
  • Hide complexity behind jargon
  • Use a template where your process needs something different

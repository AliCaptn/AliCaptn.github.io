Our approach

We begin with the decision you need to make, then work back to the data. Before building, you approve the layout and logic. After building, we check every figure against your source and hand over documentation your team can use without us.

We are an independent practice, so you work directly with the people who build your solution.

  1. Discover

    We learn your goals, decisions and data sources.

  2. Analyze

    We assess data quality and define the KPIs that matter.

  3. Design

    You approve layout and logic before we build.

  4. Develop

    We build, validate and test against your source data.

  5. Deliver

    Handover, walkthrough and documentation for your team.

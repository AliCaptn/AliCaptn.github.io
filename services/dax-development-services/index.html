Questions about Advanced DAX Development

What is DAX?

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) is the formula language used in Power BI to create measures and calculated columns, such as year-over-year growth or running totals.

Can you fix measures that give wrong results?

Yes. We trace the logic against your source data, find where the result drifts, then correct and document the measure.

Questions about KPI & Executive Reporting Solutions

How many KPIs should an executive report show?

Fewer is better. We usually help teams settle on a short list tied to decisions, then support the rest with drill-down detail.

Can you build a board pack?

Yes. We can design a recurring executive report pack with a scorecard, summary tables and trend charts.

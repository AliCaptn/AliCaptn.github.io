Questions about Microsoft Excel Dashboard Development

Is Excel still a good choice for dashboards?

Yes, for smaller teams and simpler data. It is familiar and quick to share. When data grows or many people need live access, Power BI is usually a better fit, and we can help you move.

Can you clean up our existing workbook?

Yes. We can simplify formulas, separate data from presentation and add clear charts without changing how your team works.
